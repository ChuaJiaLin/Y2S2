# Reflection

The Professional Communication Skills 1 course provided valuable exposure to essential academic and professional communication skills, particularly in report writing, critical reading, group discussions, and oral presentations. The course established a strong foundation for effective communication in both academic and professional contexts.

Throughout the course, significant improvement was achieved in analysing academic texts, synthesizing information, paraphrasing, summarizing, and constructing logical and systematic arguments. These skills were developed through a combination of individual and group-based assessments, enabling a more structured and critical approach to handling information.

The report writing project played an important role in enhancing critical thinking, research ability, and professional writing skills. Emphasis on the use of formal language, cohesion, transition markers, and proper referencing techniques strengthened the ability to produce well-organized and academically sound written work.

In addition, group discussions and oral presentations contributed to the development of teamwork, communication, confidence, and presentation skills. Collaborative analysis of topics and delivering presentations to different audiences improved the ability to express ideas clearly and effectively in both formal and interactive settings.

The course also increased awareness of the importance of effective communication, critical analysis, and ethical writing practices in academic and professional environments, highlighting the need for accuracy, clarity, and integrity in all forms of communication.

Overall, the course successfully enhanced both written and spoken communication skills, while improving the ability to present ideas in a clear, professional, and systematic manner.
